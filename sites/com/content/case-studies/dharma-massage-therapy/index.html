Dharma Massage Therapy

Therapeutic massage · United States · dharmamassagetherapy.com · Score 96.3 · TTI 779 ms

The problem

A high-end medical massage practice whose site did not reflect the quality of the work, ranking for generic "spa massage" terms instead of premium medical keywords.

The solution

StoryBrand rewrite, repositioning toward medical-grade bodywork, SEO for neuromuscular therapy and medical massage therapist, Google Business Profile and booking.
